## Do not write (Meta Employment-category triggers)

Avoid in copy and imagery: hiring, now hiring, join our team, apply now, careers, positions available, work from home, be your own boss, earn $X per month, commission potential, no experience needed, full/part-time, opportunity of a lifetime — and any office, handshake, headset, or "we're hiring" imagery.

Use instead: add a product, for licensed agents, integration, program, your agency, your book of business.
